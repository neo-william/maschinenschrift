The statement is made on three axes — Wortlaut (*wording*), *Geist*, Beleg (*record*) — and stands as a seal near the byline. Inclusion in the [registry](/registry/) happens by self-declaration: no fee, no review of the claim, only of its internal consistency.

The longer story lives under [the idea](/idea/); how the project came about and where it stands among other approaches, under [decisions](/decisions/).
