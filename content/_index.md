A machine does not write. It delivers language. The one who writes is the one who chooses what to keep — and the seal says how much came from the machine.

The seal reads along three axes. **Wortlaut** — *wording* — is the surface: the sentences, the cadence, the choice of words. ***Geist*** is the work's spirit — its ideas, its arguments, its structural decisions, its factual material, its content. **Beleg** — the *record* — notes whether the work stands in the bibliographic world: findable, datable, identifiable. Dots in the upper rows count what the machine contributed; a half-stroke below means the record is still pending. An empty seal means the author wrote alone.

You set your own seal — [the generator](/seal/) delivers it as SVG or PNG — and place it near the byline. To appear in the [registry](/registry/), [send a short note](/submit/) — no fee, no review of your claim, only of its internal consistency. The longer story lives under [the idea](/idea/); the why under [motivation](/motivation/).
